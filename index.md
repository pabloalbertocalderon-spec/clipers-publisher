# Clipers Publisher

Clipers Publisher is a workflow for preparing, reviewing and scheduling short-form video clips across connected social channels.

Account owners authorize each social account separately. Clips are reviewed before publication. Connections use the permissions granted by each platform and can be revoked from that platform's account settings.

The TikTok integration is under development. Public posting through TikTok has not been approved or enabled.

## How the workflow works

1. Prepare a clip from source material the operator is authorized to use.
2. Review its edits, captions and publication details.
3. Authorize the intended social account.
4. Schedule or publish using the connected platform's available features.
