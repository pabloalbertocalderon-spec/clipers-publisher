# Privacy policy — Clipers Publisher

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

## What the integration uses

Connecting a social account involves the platform's authorization process. Depending on the permissions granted, Clipers Publisher and its local Postiz installation can access account identifiers, profile details, authorization tokens, uploaded media, publication information and channel analytics. Some platforms request broader management permissions; these are shown on their authorization screen.

The local Postiz installation stores connection credentials and publication data on the operator's computer. Videos and publication details are transmitted to the relevant social platform when an upload or publication is requested. Each platform processes that information under its own policies.

## Informational website

These informational pages have no application sign-in form, advertising trackers or analytics scripts added by Clipers. The hosting provider may process technical request information under its own privacy policy.

## Your control

An account owner can revoke the app's access from the connected platform's account settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete data already stored locally or content already posted on the social platform. Those actions require separate deletion by the operator or account owner.
