# Terms of use — Clipers Publisher

Last updated: October 14, 2026.

## Purpose and current status

Clipers Publisher supports preparation, review and scheduling of short video clips for accounts authorized by their owners. The TikTok integration is in development and has not been approved for public Direct Post. The project does not guarantee publication availability, audience reach, virality or earnings.

## Content and account authorization

The operator must have the necessary rights or permissions for source videos, music and other material. Account owners authorize each connection separately and retain control over what is published. Campaign conditions and each platform's rules apply to every publication.

## Reviewing publications

Review the clip, caption, destination account and any commercial-content disclosure before publishing. Scheduling or platform access does not establish compliance with a campaign's conditions or grant rights to source content.

## Availability

Integrations depend on platform permissions, quotas, reviews, account status and the availability of the local installation. A successful connection does not guarantee that a particular upload or public publication will succeed.
